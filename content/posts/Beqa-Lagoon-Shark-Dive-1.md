## Beqa Lagoon shark dive

We were on Viti Levu in Fiji, on our way out to Plantation Island in the Mamanuca island chain of the west coast. We'd come up to Fiji for an extended family birthday celebration holiday. But we came a day ahead of the wider group in order for Leo and I to take in the shark dive at Beqa Lagoon. 

We were so glad we did.
<!-- excerpt -->

The dive day was beautifully run by Beqa Adventure Divers, we felt perfectly safe and they were mindful of both the animal's welfare and our impact on the environment. 

All in all it was a great diving experience. And just as advertised, absolutely *littered* with sharks.

We did two dives that day, this is a highlights video from the first - enjoy!

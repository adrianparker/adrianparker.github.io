Fat Freddy's Drop are like a comfortable pair of audio slippers. They're named after a stronger than usual batch of LSD that was popular in Wellington around the time they formed, so they've always been part of Wellington music folklore. This was my third time seeing them on record, after London Astoria in 2007 and The Opera House in 2013, plus nights at the Matterhorn I only dustily remember in that period when records weren't kept.

<!-- excerpt -->

It was 21 years since their big record **Based On A True Story**, and they marked it with three nights at the Michael Fowler Centre. Three nights! That's huge for Wellington, and testimony to their old school roots. It's always nice to be in the group that bought their tickets fast. My partner in crime Jo and I caught the first.

The crowd was out for a good night, there to relive the memories of that time just as much as the record. It was unusually smoky upstairs, and the acoustics were top drawer.

They played the album front to back, at least all the tracks I remember, with a couple of extras mixed into the longer jams. The band were tight, although some of them are not originals, and Dallas Tamaira's vocals were just as sweet and soulful as back in the day.

There were overt references to Mu, the DJ in the original band, who died last July. It was tastefully done, not overly sad, but with huge aroha.

A couple of songs went from indulgent to meandering, which happens sometimes with jam bands. It never got truly boring, but some moments dragged.

Overall Wellington had a good old walk down memory lane, the band were top drawer, and we paid our respects. Comfy slippers indeed.

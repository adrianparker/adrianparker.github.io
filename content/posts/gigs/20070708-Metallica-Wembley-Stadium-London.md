OMFG. Just in case there was any doubt, Metallica know how to rock!

<!-- excerpt -->

The day after Live Earth, back at Wembley Stadium, this time for the real thing.

First up was Machine Head. Very loud, but too messy - their sound was just a big wash of growl and if there was a tune in there somewhere, it got hidden. Just before the main act came H.I.M., who appeared to have tunes but got booed off the stage. Their cover of **Wicked Game** by Chris Isaak was cool though.

Then it was showtime, and what a show. These guys have a wicked rapport with the crowd, and Jo was blown away by it. They played most of the songs I wanted to hear. During one of the newer ones - I think it was **The Memory Remains** - James Hetfield got the crowd to sing the melody. Alone. For about two minutes straight. So loud. The band were visibly stoked by the response.

It was a very powerful performance that lived up to everything I'd ever hoped this band would be. Thanks to Jo and D for rocking with me, especially D who paid the price!

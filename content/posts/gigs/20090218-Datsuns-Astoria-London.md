The Datsuns have always been a band that sounds better loud.

<!-- excerpt -->

All I remember from this show is that the grungy, dirty, no frills balls to the wall sound of The Datsuns in full force matched the venue perfectly. It was Underworld in Camden, and the **Headstunts** tour.

German rock legends Scorpions whistled up a treat of a show.

<!-- excerpt -->

One of my very first metal cassettes was "Masters of Metal", a compilation of many rock and metal hits frm the mid 80s. On that tape was the epic tune **Rock You Like A Hurricane** and I adored it - that epic opening, the soaring vocals, it remained just the classiest of epic tunes.

So, 20 years later, despite their being near retirement age already, I jumped at the chance to see them live. They played Hammersmith Apollo, the iconic venue not too far from where we lived, and they rocked up a storm. Absolute class. The singer wore his signature little leather cap, and they played all my favourites, of course including **Winds of Change**, complete with the whistling. 

Huge band, epic set, great gig.

Featuring the same lineup as iconic album **Last Splash**. 

<!-- excerpt -->

The first time I saw the Breeders was supporting Smashing Pumpkins in Wellington. Then I saw them at a festival, or two, I think, and then shamefully I had a ticket to see them in San Francisco, but managed to think the show was over, when in fact they hadn't even started. A jazz DJ I'd met at the hostel earlier that evening must take some of the blame for that one!

Anyway, a few years later, with the original lineup again, including both Deal sisters, not arguing too much - how could we not? And at Koko t00 - a real treat, a lovely, plush, crushed velvet, neon lights and models doing coke in the bathroom kinda joint.

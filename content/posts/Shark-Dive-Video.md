## Shark diving at Beqa Lagoon

We were on Viti Levu in Fiji a day ahead of everyone else in order to take in the shark dive at Beqa Lagoon. We were so glad we did. It was beautifully run by Beqa Adventure Divers, safe and environmentally careful, a great diving experience. Absolutely littered in sharks.
